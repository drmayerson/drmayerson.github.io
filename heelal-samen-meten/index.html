What is simulated

  • Simulated universe. Each data set (one per era) has a secret H0, drawn uniformly in 64–76 km/s/Mpc when it is reset (the facilitator can reveal it). Flat ΛCDM with Ωm = 0.315 (Planck 2018) fixes the shape of dL(z); only H0 is fitted.
  • LIGO–Virgo era: binary neutron stars with a kilonova (bright sirens), uniform in volume between 15 and 160 Mpc. Fractional distance error σ = 0.15 + 0.10·(d/160 Mpc), log-normal (mostly the distance–inclination degeneracy; GW170817 had 43.8 +2.9/−6.9 Mpc).
  • Einstein Telescope era: 65 % bright sirens at z ≤ 0.5 (kilonova reach), 35 % dark sirens (binary black holes, no counterpart) at z ≤ 1, both drawn ∝ dVc/dz/(1+z). σ = 0.08 + 0.12·z/0.5 (bright) or 0.10 + 0.10 z (dark), plus weak lensing σlens = 0.066[(1−(1+z)−1/4)/0.25]1.8 (Tamanini et al. 2016) in quadrature. Stylized: real ET errors depend on network, sky position, orientation.
  • Peculiar velocities: 300 km/s Gaussian added to every galaxy redshift; included in the fit weights as (300 km/s)/(cz).
  • Dark sirens: 7 candidate galaxies spread through the GW distance range; the true host is chosen ∝ brightness. A wrong pick gives a wrong redshift, as in reality. Their distance error is inflated ×1.5 in the fit to account for that. (Real dark-siren analyses weight all candidates statistically instead of picking one.)

What the visitor does, and the fit

  • Distance: the visitor scales a template until its amplitude matches the (noisy) data. Amplitude ∝ 1/dL; the chirp's masses are taken as known. The visitor's distance is what they choose, so sloppy matching adds real scatter. Waveform display and audio are simplified (leading-order chirp).
  • Redshift: the visitor drags laboratory lines (Hα, Hβ, [O III], [O II], Ca H&K, Mg b, Na D, …) over the galaxy spectrum on a log-wavelength axis, where a redshift is a pure shift. On "done" the computer refines within ±0.006 in ln(1+z) by cross-correlation and requires a minimum match.
  • Fit: weighted mean of ln H0,i = ln[c·D(zi)/di], D the dimensionless luminosity distance, weights 1/(σd,i² + σv,i²). Error 1/√Σw. Shown with Planck 2018 (67.4 ± 0.5) and SH0ES (Riess et al. 2022, 73.04 ± 1.04).
  • Shared: points are stored in this browser (localStorage), so one stand device collects the whole day. Several devices can be merged with export/import. A live multi-device version needs a small backend.
