    Method

    • Pitch track: YIN (threshold 0.15) on the microphone audio decimated to 16 kHz, 10 ms hop, 65–3000 Hz; median-cleaned; longest voiced stretch kept. Loudness gate max(0.004, 8 % of the 98th-percentile frame RMS, min(30 % of it, 2 × room level measured before the press)).
    • Track fit (free-mode score): leading-order (0PN) chirp f(t) = (1/π)(5/256)3/8(G𝓜/c³)−5/8(tc−t)−3/8. For each tc on a grid, ln 𝓜 is solved by least squares in ln f; the best tc wins. Score = mean over frames of exp(−½(Δ/1 semitone)²).
    • Karaoke score: target = last 1.0 → 0.05 s of the 0PN chirp of the event (GW170817 at real speed; GW150914 with 𝓜/25). Mean over 10 ms target samples of exp(−½(Δ/2 semitones)²), unvoiced = 0; best over time shifts ±0.4 s and whole-octave shifts −2…+2.
    • Coherent overlap (the real matched filter): normalised, phase-maximised overlap of the raw audio with complex 0PN templates (amplitude ∝ f2/3), 𝓜 within ±15 % of the track fit. Shown here only.
    • Tape speed: black-hole mode treats the voice as the wave sped up 25× in time and frequency together, so all masses scale by 25 (exact symmetry of vacuum GR; not exact for neutron-star matter).
    • Masses: the chirp measures 𝓜 only. Component masses are shown for q = 1 (m = 21/5𝓜). Voice-frame 𝓜 is treated as source-frame. Classes: < 1.0 M☉ each “lighter than any known neutron star”; 1.0–2.5 neutron stars; 2.5–5 mass gap; > 5 black holes.
    • Reach: same definition and tables as ET-horizon: LIGO-Virgo-KAGRA network at measured O4 sensitivity and ET-D triangle, SNR ≥ 10, redshift where half of the sources (random sky position and orientation) are detected. Generated by dev/gen-reach.mjs from et-horizon/index.html; 𝓜 < 1 extrapolated with SNR ∝ ((1+z)𝓜)5/6/DL, 𝓜 > 120 with the mass dependence of the earlier PhenomA horizon. Planck 2018 cosmology. Above z = 20 the ET line says “before the first stars (formal limit)”.
    • Audio of templates and events: simplified waveforms built from published masses (0PN inspiral, PhenomA merger and ringdown frequencies). Simulated, not detector data.
