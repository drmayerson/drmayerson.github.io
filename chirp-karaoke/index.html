For physicists: what is computed, and what to sign off

This chirp

Method

  • Pitch track: YIN (threshold 0.15) on the microphone audio, 10 ms hop, 65–3000 Hz; median-cleaned; longest voiced stretch kept.
  • Track fit (headline score): leading-order (0PN) chirp f(t) = (1/π)(5/256)3/8(G𝓜/c³)−5/8(tc−t)−3/8. For each tc on a grid, ln 𝓜 is solved by least squares in ln f; the best tc wins. Score = mean over frames of exp(−½(Δ/1 semitone)²).
  • Coherent overlap (the real matched filter): normalised, phase-maximised overlap of the raw microphone audio with complex 0PN templates (amplitude ∝ f2/3), FFT over all time shifts, 𝓜 within ±15 % of the track fit. Harmonics of the voice and pitch wobble push this far below the track score. That gap is the lesson.
  • Tape speed: real-speed mode uses the voice as is. Black-hole mode treats the voice as the GW sped up 25× in time and frequency together, so all masses scale by 25 (exact symmetry of vacuum GR; not exact for neutron-star matter).
  • Masses: the chirp measures 𝓜 only. Component masses are shown for q = 1 (m = 21/5𝓜). Voice-frame 𝓜 is treated as source-frame (no redshift correction).
  • Reach: SNR = 8 horizon for an optimally oriented, overhead source (best case; sky/orientation-averaged range is ≈2.26× closer at low z). Amplitude: PhenomA (Ajith et al. 2008) for black holes; inspiral-only to ISCO when components < 3 M☉. LIGO: one detector, O4 design curve (190 Mpc BNS range; our code reproduces 189 Mpc). ET: ET-D, triangle = 3 × 60° interferometers → SNR × 3/2. Cosmology: Planck 2018 (H₀ = 67.66, Ωm = 0.310). Distances shown as look-back time.
  • Audio of templates and events: simplified waveforms built from published masses (0PN inspiral, PhenomA merger and ringdown frequencies). Simulated, not detector data.

Please sign off

  • Tape-speed factor 25 for black-hole mode.
  • Equal-mass split for the headline masses, and the class boundaries (lighter than any known NS < 1.0 M☉ each; neutron stars 1.0–2.5; mass gap 2.5–5; black holes > 5).
  • Match-score width (1 semitone). Tests: a pure chirp scores ~99 %, an accelerating glide ~84 %, a linear glide ~45 %.
  • Real-speed mode puts most human whoops below 1 M☉ per object (slow glides = light objects), so many visitors will get the “lighter than any known neutron star / primordial black hole?” result. Black-hole mode gives 10–40 M☉ black holes for the same whoops.
  • Reach as best-case horizon (not averaged range), single LIGO detector at O4 design, ET-D instead of the 2023 ET curves, and the jump at 3 M☉ where the amplitude model switches.
  • Wording "before the first stars" when the ET horizon exceeds z ≈ 20.
