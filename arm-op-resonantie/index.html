    How much does the animation above exaggerate?

    Not to scale. Real mirrors drift by up to around a micrometre, hundreds of times the width of the resonance (about a nanometre), and the real lock holds them far more tightly than shown. Here the resonance is drawn much wider and the motion much smaller so you can see both at once. The gravitational wave is exaggerated even more: a real one changes the 10 km arm by less than a hundredth of the width of a proton. In the real detector the wave stretches one arm while squeezing the other, and that difference is what gets read out.
